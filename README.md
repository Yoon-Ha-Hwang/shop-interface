# How it works
First you will add products you want to buy by clicking on the Add to Cart buttons. Then on the next page, you will see a sign showing how much of each product is in your cart, and how much they cost altogether. Then there's the option to use store credit. You will type how much you store credit you want to spend (there's practically no limit to how much store credit you can use). If you still need to pay after the store credits there is the currency exchange feature where you will enter card information to pay. After that you will be displayed a receipt of your purchase.

# One Line Summary / Explain Like I am Five / TLDR / etc.
You can buy made-up products from the made-up store and pay using made-up information, although obviously you don't get the actual product(s). 

# Why I built it
On social media, I saw a post where people build fake shopping websites so people can get dopamine for the sensation of shopping (i.e. spending money makes them happy). But they made fake sites because shopping those products in real life (I mean, the lineup includes things like luxurious designer bags) would cost thousands of dollars which means it's not something they can do all day. That made me get interested in how people design digital storefronts.
